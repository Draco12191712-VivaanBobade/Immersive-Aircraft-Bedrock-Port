# <span style="color: #f6a75f;">Immersive Aircraft, Now on Bedrock!<br></span>

   <span style="color: #fff;">Immersive Aircraft: Bedrock Port (Official) brings all seven vanilla-style aircraft from <span style="color: #3598db;"><a style="color: #3598db;" href="https://www.curseforge.com/members/conczin/projects" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Conczin's</a></span></span> <span style="color: #fff;"> Java Edition Mod, <span style="color: #fbeeb8;"><a style="color: #fbeeb8;" href="https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/immersive-aircraft" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Immersive Aircraft,</a></span> directly to Bedrock! All of it! Their physics, multiplayer support, fuel, upgrades, weapons, models, animations, and sounds! It is a full port made with Conczin's permission.<em>&nbsp;<span style="color: #eccafa;">Vibrant Visuals</span></em><span style="color: #eccafa;">&nbsp;<span style="color: #fff;">isn't required, but there are <em><span style="color: #eccafa;">VV</span></em> features in the pack.&nbsp;</span></span></span> 
 

## <span style="color: #34495e;">Aircraft</span>

<span style="color: #ecf0f1;">Several aircraft have been ported, and each has its own <strong><span style="color: #3b4875;">unique</span></strong> features!&nbsp;</span> 

### <span style="color: #3b4875;">Biplane</span>

<span style="color: #fff;">The Biplane has one seat. It's powered by a Boiler (furnace fuel). It has 16 cargo slots, 1 weapon slot, and 4 upgrade slots. It needs a runway to take off and has a booster slot for fireworks.</span>

### <span style="color: #3b4875;">Gyrodyne</span>

<span style="color: #fff;">This has 2 seats and is powered by, well, nothing! (Okay, it's actually powered by your food, so bring snacks.) It has 18 cargo slots, 1 weapon slot, and 3 upgrade slots. Hold the W key to spin the rotor, then lift off. (For mobile players, hold the Forward button / Joystick forward to spin the rotor)</span>

### <span style="color: #3b4875;">Quadrocopter</span>

<span style="color: #fff;">This copter has 1 seat and is powered by 1 boiler. It has 6 cargo slots, 1 weapon slot, &amp; 1 upgrade slot. This mainly hovers in place and moves very precisely. It is built from bamboo.</span>

### <span style="color: #3b4875;">Airship</span>

<span style="color: #fff;">The Airship has 2 seats, is powered by a boiler, and is easy to fly. It has 16 cargo slots, 1 weapon slot, &amp; 3 upgrade slots. It is slow &amp; steady, can climb altitude vertically (can move straight up), and has dyeable sails &amp; banners!</span>

### <span style="color: #3b4875;">Cargo Airship</span>

<span style="color: #fff;">This huge cargo ship has 2 seats, runs on a single boiler, and has <strong>80 cargo slots</strong> (all on one screen), 0 weapon slots, &amp; 4 upgrade slots. It's basically a huge flying warehouse, with, again, dyeable sails &amp; banners!</span>

### <span style="color: #3b4875;">Warship</span>

<span style="color: #fff;">This beast is the only thing you need for war. And that too, the best thing! It has 4 seats, needs 3 boilers, 66 cargo slots, 2 weapon slots, and 6 upgrade slots. The pilot can fly while gunners aim turrets at things. Has 3x the hull strength &amp; a booster slot.</span>

### <span style="color: #3b4875;">Bamboo Hopper</span>

<span style="color: #fff;">This ship has 3 seats and only needs one boiler. It has 16 cargo slots, 2 weapon slots, and 4 upgrade slots. It's a seaplane made of, well, you guessed it, bamboo. It can float and take off from water. It also has a booster slot!</span>

## <span style="color: #3598db;">Controls</span>

<span style="color: #fff;">Everything works the same on touch, controller, and keyboard: you fly with the movement stick &amp; where you look. Planes: push the stick forward for throttle, and the nose follows where you look. Copters &amp; airships: look up to climb and down to descend. Click (or tap anywhere on mobile) to fire weapons, and click the telescope once to zoom in &amp; again to zoom out. Sneak (or the dismount button) to get out.</span>

<span style="color: #fff;">To open an aircraft's Java-style inventory, press your inventory key while seated, or sneak next to it &amp; press Use (the Open button on mobile).</span>

<span style="color: #fff;">New in the <strong>Flight Camera Update (1.2.0)</strong>: you tilt with the aircraft, your FOV widens as you speed up, and you can switch to free look (stick-only steering) to look around while you fly!</span>

## <span style="color: #f6a75f;">Settings &amp; the aircraft panel</span>

<span style="color: #e67e23;"><strong>World settings</strong>&nbsp;<span style="color: #fff;">live in Settings &gt; Behavior Packs &gt; Immersive Aircraft &gt; the gear icon. Speed, lift, fuel, wind, crashes, weapons, repair, drops, and the flight camera, all per world. No commands needed!</span></span>

<span style="color: #e67e23;"><strong>Sneak + hit</strong>&nbsp;<span style="color: #fff;">an aircraft to open its panel (don't worry, it never damages the aircraft). Condition, fuel, ammo, upgrade summary stuff. Slot manager, boost, steering mode, flight camera effects, the 3rd-person camera toggle, kicking out passengers, and a controls page as well.</span></span>

**<span style="color: #e67e23;">/ia:dev&nbsp;</span>** <span style="color: #fff;">is for operators with cheats on. It has every option from the original mod's config, plus buttons to give one of every aircraft or every upgrade &amp; weapon.&nbsp;</span> 

## <span style="color: #eccafa;"><em>Vibrant</em> <em>Visuals</em></span>

<span style="color: #fff;">All textures in this add-on have MERS &amp; normal maps. With <span style="color: #eccafa;"><em>Vibrant Visuals</em></span> on, metal parts (copper, steel, engines, and gauges) shine, everything else (wood, canvas, bamboo, and glass) stays matte, surfaces have real depth, and the Cargo Airship's furnace glows!</span>

## <span style="color: #e03e2d;">Differences from Java Mod</span>

<span style="color: #fff;">There are some differences from the original mod that I may add to Bedrock later. But for now, the engine has a few limits compared to what Java Edition can do.</span>

<span style="color: #fff;">You tilt with the aircraft, but the first-person camera doesn't roll (Bedrock doesn't let add-ons roll the camera). The cockpit gauges and HUD are shown as action bar text instead of drawn instruments. Banners show their base color, not their patterns. The telescope is click to zoom in &amp; click again to zoom out, since Bedrock can't tell add-ons when a button is held. Aircraft are also a bit faster by default, so set the speed multiplier &amp; plane lift assist to 1 in the world settings for the exact Java feel.</span>
